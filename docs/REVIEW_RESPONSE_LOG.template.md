# Technical feedback and response log — private template

This is a blank record for real feedback, not evidence that anyone has
reviewed the project. Keep private contact details out of a public release.
Ask permission before naming a reviewer or quoting their words. A correction
or conversation is not an endorsement or recommendation letter.

The strongest record is a dated, specific criticism followed by a
reproducible correction and a candid remaining limit. Do not use a person's
affiliation as a substitute for their actual assessment of the work.
