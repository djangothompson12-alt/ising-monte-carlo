How to read the comparisons

To test resolution, compare a processed length with the native length of the same crop. To test field size, compare native crop rows with the full box. A ratio of 1 means unchanged measured length. A missing crossing stays unresolved. Chords exclude boundary-touching runs and may therefore favour shorter features in small fields.
